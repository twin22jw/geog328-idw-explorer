Group discussion questions

Change one factor at a time. Compare the maps, click a prediction location to inspect sample weights, and explain what you observe.

  1. 1. Power αChange α from 1 to 8. How do the prediction and sample weights change at the same location? Why?
  2. 2. Search neighborhoodCompare all samples, a fixed radius, and the nearest k samples. Which observations contribute to a prediction? When do blank areas appear?
  3. 3. Sampling strategy and countCompare systematic, random, and clustered sampling. Where would you trust the predictions least, and why? What happens when you reduce the number of samples?
  4. 4. BarrierTurn on the barrier and move it. How do the predictions and sample weights change near the barrier? Why?
  5. 5. Shape of neighborhoodCompare circular and elongated neighborhoods. How does the direction of the neighborhood affect which samples have greater influence?
