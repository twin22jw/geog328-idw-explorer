How is the spline calculated?

The map uses a global, exact thin-plate spline:

ẑ(x,y) = a₀ + a₁x + a₂y + Σᵢ cᵢ rᵢ² ln(rᵢ)

Here rᵢ = √((x − xᵢ)² + (y − yᵢ)²) is the distance to sample i. The coefficients are solved together from all 12 active sample values so the surface passes through each observation. At rᵢ = 0, its kernel term is defined as 0.
