What to notice

Piecewise, with smooth joins

Each interval has its own cubic expression, but the curve and its slope meet smoothly at every observation.

Exact or approximate

At λ = 0, the curve passes through all points. Smoothing trades exact fit for a gentler curve.

Outliers and edges

A single unusual value can bend nearby segments. Predictions near sparse areas and beyond the end points need care.
