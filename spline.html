Group discussion questions

Change one thing at a time. Use the maps and A–B profile as evidence in your shared Google Doc.

  1. 1D degreeHow do 1st-, 2nd-, and 3rd-degree curves differ between the same observations? What changes near the outlier?
  2. Local influenceIncrease IDW power. What changes around individual samples on the map and profile?
  3. Broad trendsMove A–B across different parts of the maps. Where does the spline rise or fall over a wider area than IDW?
  4. Range of predictionsCompare each profile with the observed minimum and maximum lines. Can either method predict beyond those values? Why might that happen?
  5. Method choiceWhere are the two predictions most different? Which pattern would you investigate before choosing a method for real data?
