How is ordinary kriging calculated?

For a constant but unknown local mean, ordinary kriging predicts ẑ(x₀) = Σᵢ λᵢz(xᵢ), with Σᵢ λᵢ = 1. The weights solve a covariance system built from the chosen variogram model and all active observations. They reflect both distance to x₀ and distances among observations.

The map uses an isotropic, stationary covariance model. The nugget is treated as independent observation noise. Kriging standard deviation is the square root of the resulting model-based prediction variance. It describes uncertainty under these assumptions; it is not a measured error or a guarantee of accuracy.
